The Round — everyone takes one turn

1Initiative leads

The player with the initiative marker plays the lead card — or passes initiative clockwise to the next player with cards, ending the round.

2Others follow

Each other player, in clockwise order, takes a turn: Surpass, Copy, or Pivot. No cards in hand? Skip.

3Check initiative

If someone seized, they keep it. Otherwise it goes to the highest Surpass. Nobody Surpassed? It stays put.

4Discard & repeat

Discard all played cards face down. Hands empty (or everyone with cards passed)? The chapter ends — score ambitions (see back).

Your Turn — Four Steps, In Order

1Play one card

As lead if you hold initiative — otherwise Surpass, Copy, or Pivot (1 below).

2Declare / Seize

Optional. The initiative player may declare an ambition; others may seize the initiative (2 below).

3Prelude

Spend any number of resources and “Prelude:” card actions (3 below).

4Take actions

One action per pip, matching your card’s suit (4 below). Your first pip ends the Prelude.

1Play Your Card — Four Ways

Lead initiative player — all pips

Play any card face up as the lead card. Take up to one action per pip in its corner. Its suit sets the round’s lead suit.

Surpass — all pips

Face up, same suit as the lead card, higher number than the lead card (not than every card!). Take up to one action per pip on your card.

Copy — 1 action

Any card, face down. Take one action of the lead card. Can’t Surpass or Pivot? You can always Copy.

Pivot — 1 action

Face up, any card not of the lead suit. Take one action on your card (not the lead card’s!). Doesn’t change the lead suit.

2Declare or Seize

Declare — only the initiative player, when playing the lead card, before Prelude & actions: cover the card’s number with the zero marker (it becomes 0, pips unchanged — easy to Surpass, so you’ll likely lose initiative) and put the highest available ambition marker in the matching box. The card’s number picks the ambition:

2 Tycoon · 3 Tyrant · 4 Warlord · 5 Keeper · 6 Empath · 7 any · 1 none

You may re-declare an already-declared ambition (stack another marker). No markers left? No declaring. (“1” and “7” cards are used in 4-player games only.)

Seize — any other player, when playing their card, if no one has seized yet this round: play an extra card face down (no actions from it), or Surpass with a 7 (4-player). Lay the marker on its side. Whoever seized keeps initiative at round’s end.

Initiative

Whoever holds the marker leads the next round — going first is power, but leading spends your good cards.

Passing: with initiative and no card you want to play, pass it clockwise (to the next player holding cards) — the round ends immediately.

3Prelude — What Resources Do

Material — take a Build or Repair action.

Fuel — take a Move action.

Weapon — this turn, your card’s pips may also be spent on Battle actions.

Relic — take a Secure action.

Psionic — take any action listed on the lead card.

Spent resources return to the supply only after your Prelude ends, and cards secured this Prelude can’t be used yet.

Outrage: Outraged resources cannot be spent for their Prelude action — they still count for ambitions and can still be taxed. You also can’t spend resources if you pass initiative or have no cards in hand.

Tax — pick a Loyal city, or a Rival city you control: gain 1 resource of its planet type. Rival city: also capture 1 of their agents. Once per city per turn.

Build — place a city/starport in an empty slot on a planet with Loyal pieces, or 1 ship at a Loyal starport (1 ship per starport per turn). Built where a Rival controls? It arrives damaged.

Move — any number of Loyal ships, one system » one adjacent system. From a Loyal starport: Catapult — keep moving until entering any planet or a gate a Rival controls.

Repair — flip or stand up 1 damaged Loyal piece, anywhere on the map.

Influence — place 1 agent on any card in the Court.

Secure — take a Court card where you have more agents than each Rival. Your agents return; Rival agents become Captives. Refill the Court.

Battle — fight in one system (full workflow on back).

Battle — an Aggression action
1

Choose battle system

Any one system with your Loyal ships — they are your attackers.

2

Choose defender

One Rival with pieces there. All their pieces in the system defend.

3

Collect dice — up to 1 per attacking ship

Mix freely; max 6 of a type; you may take fewer. Assault hits fast but singes you. Skirmish is slow and safe. Raid steals and cracks buildings — riskiest.

Raid limit: raid dice only if the defender has buildings here — or no Loyal buildings anywhere on the map.

4

Roll — resolve every face, in this order

1 · Hit your own ships — once per flame rolled.

2 · Intercept — if any rings rolled: hit your ships once per fresh defending ship. Happens at most once per battle.

3 · Hit defending ships — once per burst. No defending ships left? Hit their buildings instead.

4 · Hit defending buildings — once per building-burst.

5 · Raid — only if you still have attacking ships: spend keys to steal the defender’s resources & Guild cards, paying each item’s raid cost in keys.

The attacker makes every choice — the defender just watches.

Resolving a Battle & Triggering Outrage

fresh » damaged » destroyed. A hit damages a fresh piece (tip ship / flip building) and destroys a damaged one.

Trophies: destroyed pieces land in a Trophies box — the attacker takes the defender’s destroyed pieces, the defender takes the attacker’s. Trophies feed Warlord.

Destroying a city (even outside battle):

1 · Provoke Outrage — discard all your resources & Guild cards of that city’s planet type; an agent covers that Outrage slot. That resource type loses its Prelude action for the rest of the game.

2 · Ransack the Court — immediately Secure any Court card holding the defender’s agents (any number). Rival agents there become Trophies, not Captives.

Pieces & Terms

Ship — moves, battles, and controls systems.

City — tax it for resources; opens resource slots & scoring bonuses.

Starport — builds ships; launches Catapult moves.

Agent — influence in the Court; can be captured.

Initiative marker — its holder leads the round; laid on its side when seized.

Ambition marker — marks a declared ambition and the Power it awards.

Loyal — a piece of your color. Rival — another player, or a piece of their color.

Control — more fresh ships in the system than each Rival. On a tie, no one controls.

Fresh — upright ship, or building on its bright side. Damaged — tipped ship, or building on its dark side. Hitting a damaged piece destroys it.

Building — a city or a starport.

System — a planet or a gate. Systems sharing a thin border are adjacent.

Gate — the numbered hub system of each 3-planet cluster. It is adjacent to its cluster’s planets and to the neighboring clusters’ gates, so travel between clusters flows through gates. Gates have no building slots.

Capture — take a Rival piece to your Captives box (feeds Tyrant).

Trophy — a destroyed piece: the attacker takes the defender’s destroyed pieces and vice versa (feeds Warlord).

The Battle Dice — all six faces
Assault

Heavy damage, self-hits, one intercept face

Skirmish

Half hit, half nothing — never hurts you

Raid

Keys & building hits — but self-hits and intercepts

Hit a defending ship (then buildings)

Self-hit — hit your own attacking ship

Hit building — a defending building

Intercept — defender strikes back once

Key — spend to steal (raid cost)

Pip — one action from a played card

Chapter End — Scoring & Victory

Score each declared ambition: 1st place gains the large number on every marker in its box; 2nd place gains the small. You must have at least one of what it counts.

Ties: tied for 1st » each tied player takes 2nd place Power, 1st goes unawarded, and everyone else scores nothing — 2nd is used up. Tied for 2nd » those tied score nothing.

City bonus: each untied 1st gains +2 if your “+2” city slot is open (+5 if “+3” is open too).

each chapter, lowest unflipped marker flips

Then return markers (and scored Trophies/Captives), and everyone draws a new hand of 6 cards. Game ends at chapter end if anyone has 27 Power (4p) / 30 (3p) / 33 (2p) — or after Chapter 5. Most Power wins; ties go to the tied player earliest in turn order.

The Court & Guild Cards

Influence puts agents on Court cards; Secure takes one where you have the most. Guild cards join your play area — their suit icon counts toward ambitions exactly like a resource, and their raid cost is the keys needed to steal them in battle. Vox cards resolve immediately when secured.

Leaders & Lore

Your Leader grants unique powers all game. Lore cards grant persistent powers — no suit icon or raid cost, so they never add to ambitions and can’t be stolen. Card text beats the rulebook; only “cannot” is absolute. New actions like “Summon (Build)” may replace the named action whenever you could take it.
